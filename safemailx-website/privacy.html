Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to TrustMail AI. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application. Our core philosophy is Zero Data Retention.

2. Data Collection and Use

TrustMail AI connects to your Gmail account using OAuth to scan emails for phishing attempts. All analysis is performed by the TrustMail AI server and workers.

  • Email Content: We do not store your email content, attachments, or metadata. Once an email is scanned and a verdict is generated, the email data is immediately purged from our active memory.
  • OAuth Tokens: We securely encrypt and store your OAuth tokens to provide continuous background scanning. These tokens are used strictly to read incoming emails and send forensic reports.
  • User Accounts: We store your email address and a securely hashed password to manage your TrustMail AI account.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Period.

4. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (AES-256 for tokens, PBKDF2 for passwords) to secure your credentials. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

5. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us at privacy@trustmail.ai.
