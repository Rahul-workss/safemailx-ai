Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Zero Data Retention

1. Our Privacy Commitment

SafeMail X AI is architected from the ground up on the principle of Local-First Intelligence and Zero Data Retention. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or harvest your personal communications, call recordings, identity documents, or financial data. Period.

2. Multi-Channel Data Handling

  • Voice & Call Audio: When using the Call Analyzer, audio recordings or transcripts are processed in volatile RAM using Whisper and local Qwen3-8B AI to assess scam risk. Audio files are permanently discarded immediately after score generation. No audio is ever stored or transmitted to external advertisers.
  • Aadhaar & Identity Verification: Aadhaar Secure QR codes are decrypted and cryptographically verified 100% offline using embedded UIDAI public certificates. We make zero calls to UIDAI central servers, and zero demographic data (name, DOB, address, photo) is ever saved to disk or persistent databases.
  • UPI & QR Code Scanning: Payment QR parameters (VPA, payee, merchant codes) are audited purely to detect debit-traps and fraudulent impersonation. No banking credentials, PINs, or transaction histories are ever tracked.
  • Email Content (Gmail OAuth): Our Gmail integration operates strictly under a Label-Only permission model. SafeMail X AI only inspects emails explicitly tagged with the designated security label. Once an email is scanned, its content and attachments are immediately purged from active memory.
  • URLs and Text Input: Scanned URLs, SMS messages, and text samples are evaluated in real time against heuristic rules, bloom filters, and reputation databases. No personal text logs are kept.

3. Authentication & Tokens

User authentication relies on standard cryptographic tokens (JWT). Sensitive authorization credentials (such as OAuth refresh tokens) are encrypted with hardware-grade AES-256 before storage on your private instance and are used solely to deliver requested security scans.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

SafeMail X AI's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions, bug reports, or security disclosures regarding this policy, please open an issue on our official GitHub repository or contact our maintainers.
