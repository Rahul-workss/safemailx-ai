Modern cybercriminals don't just send emails. They call you pretending to be the police, send deceptive UPI QR codes, register fake telecom numbers, and forge identity documents. SafeMail X AI neutralizes every vector.

Detect phone scams by describing what the caller said or uploading call audio. Whisper transcribes speech while our 7-layer scam intelligence classifies Digital Arrests, courier parcel traps, and fake CBI/police threats in real time with Tavily policy checks.

UPI Fraud & QR Forensic Scanner

Scan QR codes with live camera or gallery screenshots. The NPCI forensic engine decodes VPAs, payee names, Merchant Category Codes (MCC), and uncovers debit-traps where attackers trick you into paying money under the guise of receiving a cashback or refund.

  • Live Camera & Gallery
  • Debit Trap Detection
  • Bank Handle Audit
  • MCC Code Decoder

Offline Aadhaar & Document Verifier

Cryptographically verify Aadhaar Secure QR codes 100% offline using embedded UIDAI public certificates and RSA-2048 verification. Decompresses big-endian gzip payloads, extracts demographic data and photo preview, with zero UIDAI calls and zero PII stored.

  • Tamper Detection
  • DigiLocker Portal Safe Route
