Total defense across all modern channels.

Modern cybercriminals don't just send emails. They call pretending to be law enforcement, generate deceptive payment QR codes, register fake telecom series, and forge identity cards. SafeMail X AI neutralizes every vector.

Call Analyzer & Vishing Defense

Detect phone scams by describing what the caller said or uploading call audio. Whisper transcribes speech while our 7-layer scam intelligence classifies Digital Arrests, courier parcel traps, and fake CBI/police threats in real time with live policy checks.

  • Whisper on-device speech transcription
  • Digital Arrest & fake police scam audit
  • Multi-lingual coverage in 12 regional languages
  • Plain-language ELI5 safety advice

Payment & QR Forensic Scanner

Scan QR codes with live camera or gallery screenshots. The forensic engine decodes payment handles, payee identity, Merchant Category Codes (MCC), and uncovers debit-traps where attackers trick you into paying money disguised as receiving a refund.

  • Dual-mode live camera & screenshot gallery inspection
  • Reverse-charge debit trap & fake refund detection
  • NPCI bank handle & verified merchant validation
  • Merchant Category Code (MCC) risk classification

Offline Aadhaar & Document Verifier

Cryptographically verify Aadhaar Secure QR codes 100% offline using embedded UIDAI public certificates and RSA-2048 verification. Decompresses big-endian gzip payloads, extracts demographic data and photo preview, with zero UIDAI calls and zero PII stored.

  • 100% offline RSA-2048 cryptographic signature check
  • UIDAI secure QR tamper & payload forgery detection
  • Direct DigiLocker official verification safe route
  • Zero cloud retention • Volatile device memory only

Who Called? Deep Profiler

Auto-detects copied numbers from your clipboard. Audits against TRAI 140/160 telemarketer regulation standards, detects international cybercrime hubs (+855 Cambodia, +95 Myanmar, +856 Laos), decodes telecom circles, and provides one-tap DOT Chakshu reporting.

  • Instant clipboard auto-detection for incoming callers
  • TRAI 140/160 telemarketer regulation audit
  • MHA cybercrime international call center hub detection
  • One-tap official DoT Chakshu portal incident filing

Email & SMS Phishing Engine

Multi-engine analysis for inbox threats. Connect via privacy-preserving Gmail OAuth (label-only model) or instant scan. Resolves deep URL redirect chains, analyzes domain age (RDAP), flags typosquatting and homoglyphs, and sandboxes documents with YARA.

  • Privacy-first Gmail OAuth with label-only inbox model
  • Instant SMS phishing & suspicious short-link audit
  • PDF and DOCX document sandboxing with YARA
  • Deep redirect-chain & domain age (RDAP) analysis
