Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

TrustMail AI helps users analyze emails for phishing, fraud, suspicious links, unsafe attachments, and other security risks. This policy explains what information we process, why we process it, and how users can request deletion.

Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, TrustMail AI may process:

  • Account information: email address, hashed password, and account settings.
  • Gmail OAuth tokens: access and refresh tokens used to connect your Gmail account.
  • Email scan data: message subject, sender, body text, forwarded email content, URLs, attachments, and extracted text needed to perform phishing analysis.
  • Scan results: verdicts, scores, analysis evidence, generated PDF reports, JSON evidence files, and scan history.
  • Device notification tokens: Expo push tokens used to send scan completion notifications if you enable notifications.
  • Operational logs: server logs needed to run, debug, and protect the service.

How We Use Gmail Data

TrustMail AI uses Gmail access only to provide user-requested email security features. The app may read messages or forwarded messages for analysis, mark processed messages when needed, and send scan or forensic replies when the user enables that workflow.

Gmail data is not used for advertising, profiling, resale, or unrelated analytics. We do not sell Gmail data or share it with third parties for marketing.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure

TrustMail AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest when production encryption is configured. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Scan reports and evidence may be stored so users can view scan history, download reports, and re-run analysis. Access to user-specific scan history is scoped to the authenticated user.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data. We may process data through infrastructure providers strictly to operate TrustMail AI, such as hosting, email delivery, push notifications, databases, and security monitoring.

User Controls and Data Deletion

You can disconnect Gmail access from your Google Account permissions page at any time. You can request deletion of your TrustMail AI account data, Gmail tokens, scan history, and reports by contacting us at privacy@safemailx-ai.tech.

Children's Privacy

TrustMail AI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the product changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact privacy@safemailx-ai.tech.
